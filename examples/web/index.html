Forager in the browser

A mapfile and GeoJSON for its layers, rendered by Forager's WebAssembly build. GeoJSON is bound to the layer whose NAME you give. The sample is a synthetic landscape that scripts/generate-example.sh generates; zoom into the tiles to see its villages, streets, and fields. The page hands the bindings the files the sample mapfile names beside it: its font set fonts.lst and the font DejaVu Sans, which its labels are drawn with.
