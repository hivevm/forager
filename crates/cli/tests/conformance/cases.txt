# The cases of msautotest's misc and renderers at the pinned release, as the conformance harness
# (crates/cli/tests/msautotest.rs) scopes them. A case is one RUN_PARMS line, or the plain map2img
# line msautotest supplies for a mapfile without one. The list names a case by its mapfile and
# its result: two lines of one mapfile that write the same result are two runs that msautotest
# compares with the same expected file, and they share one line here.
# The harness applies a line's map2img options -l, -e, and -s to the style IR, as map2img does,
# before it renders; any other option leaves the case out.
#
#   <mapfile> <result> [blank] geometry passes|fails   in scope: rendered, compared, outcome held
#   <mapfile> <result> [blank] out: <reason>           out of scope, with its reason
#
# A case is in scope when it is of the category geometry and symbols, whether Forager draws it
# right yet or not; one it draws wrong is listed as fails, with what it lacks in a comment, and
# flips to passes in the change that makes it pass. The reasons for leaving a case out are its
# category's exclusions: another output format, an OGC or CGI request, a map2img option not
# applied, a raster source, labels, a projection, a runtime substitution, a data source other
# than shapefiles; and a case MapServer itself does not reproduce within any threshold of the
# noise measurement.
#
# `blank` marks a case whose reference is nearly one colour: an image of its most common colour
# passes against it by the metric. The harness checks every mark against the reference.
#
# The threshold and the tolerance of the category geometry and symbols are those of G-6 (GEOMETRY
# in msautotest.rs), above the noise MapServer shows against its own references on CI.
